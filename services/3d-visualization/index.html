FAQ

Questions clients ask early.

Do you work outside Islamabad?

Yes. HN Architects is based in Islamabad and can consult on residential, commercial, interior, exterior, and visualization work across Pakistan.

Can you redesign an existing house?

Yes. Renovation and facade transformation work begins with existing conditions, structure, budget, and the intended lifestyle upgrade.

Do you provide 3D views before construction?

Yes. 3D visualization is part of the studio workflow so clients can understand materials, light, scale, and facade decisions before committing.

How can I start?

Send your plot size, location, current drawings if available, and your goals on WhatsApp. The studio will guide the next consultation step.
